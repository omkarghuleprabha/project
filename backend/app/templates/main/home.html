About Smart Garbage Pro

Making India Cleaner, One Step at a Time

Inspired by the Clean City Initiative, Smart Garbage Pro is a tech-driven movement dedicated to making cities cleaner through GPS fleet tracking, IoT-enabled bins, and active citizen participation.

We believe in a future where waste management is seamless, efficient, and rewarding for everyone involved — from citizens to municipal workers.
